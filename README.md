\# Pixels → Products — AI Photoshoot Studio



\*\*Track:\*\* Generative Content Workflows

\*\*Hackathon:\*\* Pixels to Products — Cloudinary AI Hackathon 2026

\*\*Live demo:\*\* https://pixels-to-products-cloudinary-ai-ha-amber.vercel.app



\## The problem



Small sellers and independent creators rarely have the budget for a proper

product photoshoot. A single plain photo (often just a phone snap on a

kitchen counter) has to do double duty as an Instagram post, a story, a

banner ad, and a marketplace listing — and it usually looks unpolished in

all four places.



\## What this does



Upload one plain product photo and instantly get back:



\- \*\*4 studio-quality scene variants\*\* — the same product placed into a

&#x20; marble counter, an outdoor lifestyle setting, a clean studio backdrop,

&#x20; and a minimal shelf scene — generated by Cloudinary's AI, not by

&#x20; manually shooting or editing anything.

\- \*\*3 ready-to-post crops\*\* — square (Instagram post), 9:16 (Story), and

&#x20; 16:9 (banner) — auto-cropped by Cloudinary's AI to keep the product

&#x20; correctly framed in every ratio.



No design skills, no photo studio, no manual editing required.



\## How Cloudinary AI powers this



This app has no custom backend or ML model of its own — every generated

image is a Cloudinary AI transformation applied directly through a URL:



\- \*\*Generative Background Replace\*\* (`e\_gen\_background\_replace`) —

&#x20; isolates the product and places it into a newly generated scene based

&#x20; on a text prompt, per scene preset.

\- \*\*AI-based Smart Cropping\*\* (`g\_auto`) — automatically detects the

&#x20; most important part of the image and keeps it in frame across very

&#x20; different aspect ratios.



Because these run as on-the-fly URL transformations, there's no upload

delay for each variant — Cloudinary generates and caches each one the

first time it's requested.



\## Tech stack



\- Next.js (App Router) + React

\- Cloudinary (unsigned client-side upload + AI transformations)

\- Deployed on Vercel



\## Try it yourself



1\. Visit the \[live demo](https://pixels-to-products-cloudinary-ai-ha-amber.vercel.app)

2\. Upload a plain product photo (a phone, a bottle, a shoe — anything

&#x20;  with a fairly simple background works best)

3\. Click "Generate shoot"

4\. Download whichever variants you like
